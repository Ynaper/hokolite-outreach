# 邮件示例 · Email Examples

示例仅作**校准**用——展示规格怎么落地、6 条要求怎么逐条满足。
**切勿直接套用**：每封邮件必须基于该达人**真实内容**独立撰写。

---

## 示例 1：EDC / 日常携带达人

**场景**：虚构 EDC 达人 **Marcus Reed**（频道 *Reed's Everyday Carry*），近期做过 Olight Baton 4 Pro 拆解、NEBO Slim 测评。
**主推产品**：HOKOLITE CLAKAP W7（磁吸工作灯）。

### Subject (primary)
`Marcus, your Baton 4 teardown raised one question`

### Subject (alternatives)
1. `Reed's Everyday Carry — a work light built for your kind of abuse`
2. `Your Olight test made us think of this one`

### Body（≈148 词）
```
Marcus, your Baton 4 Pro teardown was the first review I've seen that actually
measured pocket-clip wear — that kind of detail is rare. You've spent years
putting EDC lights like Olight and NEBO through real daily use, so a light that
survives a full work week matters to your viewers as much as the beam does.

We build HOKOLITE rechargeable lights with that same everyday-abuse mindset.
Our CLAKAP W7 folds into a magnetic work light that sticks to a toolbox or a
car hood and charges over USB-C, so it doubles as the light people reach for
when a job or a blackout hits.

For your audience, that's an easy "does it actually hold up" test — drop it in
a garage, a truck bed, a storm kit, and film what breaks. If it earns a spot in
your rotation, they'll trust it more than any spec sheet.

Open to taking a look at one?
```

### 逐条映射
| 要求 | 落点 |
|---|---|
| R1 真实姓名 | 首词即 `Marcus` |
| R2 引用真实内容 + 为什么关注 | 引用「Baton 4 Pro teardown 实测 pocket-clip 磨损」+ 理由 `that kind of detail is rare` |
| R3 产品 + 1–2 卖点，不堆参数 | 引出 CLAKAP W7；卖点=①磁吸免手（sticks to a toolbox / car hood）②USB-C + 折叠双用（job or blackout）——**未列流明/毫安数** |
| R4 达人 + 粉丝价值 | `"does it actually hold up" test` + 场景（garage / truck bed / storm kit）+ 观众价值（`trust it more than any spec sheet`） |
| R5 软邀请 | `Open to taking a look at one?` |
| R6 语言 | 美式口语（`sticks to` / `doubles as` / `film what breaks`），同行口吻，无模板词 |
| G1 | 全篇无 free sample / 付费 / 佣金 ✅ |
| G2 | ≈148 词 ✅ |
| G3 | 无链接 ✅ |
| G4 | 首词 `Marcus` ✅ |
| G5 | 引用可回指 dossier 的 source_ref ✅ |

## 示例 2：露营 / 户外达人（演示「同一产品、不同写法」）

**场景**：虚构露营达人 **Hannah Ortiz**（频道 *Campfire & Canvas*），近期做过营地灯横向对比、车顶帐篷露营系列。
**主推产品**：HOKOLITE INNOFOX C8Pro（可悬挂调光营地灯）。

### Subject (primary)
`Hannah, your camp-light roundup left one category out`

### Subject (alternatives)
1. `A lantern for the hang-and-leave-it setup in your van build`
2. `Campfire & Canvas — a light that survives a wet weekend`

### Body（≈142 词）
```
Hannah, your camp-light roundup covered beam throw and runtime well, but none
of the lanterns you tested were built to just hang and be forgotten until
morning. That gap is exactly where your van-life audience lives.

We make HOKOLITE rechargeable lighting, and our INNOFOX C8Pro is a lantern
built for that role — it hangs from a tent loop or a van ceiling, dims down to
a warm low glow for the tent, and runs long enough to leave on overnight.

For a channel that films real campsites instead of studio setups, that's a
natural test: a wet weekend, a dark site, a light left hanging all night. Your
viewers buy gear by how it behaves when it's not the star of the shot.

Worth a look?
```

### 逐条映射（要点）
- R1：首词 `Hannah`。
- R2：引用其「camp-light roundup 只测了 beam throw 和 runtime，漏了可悬挂过夜的灯」+ 为什么关注（`That gap is exactly where your van-life audience lives`）。
- R3：引出 INNOFOX C8Pro；卖点=①可悬挂（tent loop / van ceiling）②调光 + 长续航过夜——不堆参数。
- R4：`films real campsites instead of studio setups` + 场景（wet weekend / dark site / hung all night）+ 观众价值（`buy gear by how it behaves when it's not the star of the shot`）。
- R5：`Worth a look?`
- R6：同行口吻、口语化、无模板词。
- G1–G5：全部满足（≈142 词）。

> **对比两例可见**：同一个产品库、同一套规格，主题行、开头引用、卖点切入、场景、CTA 措辞**全部不同**——这就是「独立生成」而非「替换姓名」。
